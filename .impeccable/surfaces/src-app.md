## Scope

The whole GarageGo app (Expo, phone first, web secondary): Find, garage page and booking, pay sheet,
My bookings with the door, Profile, Settings, host screens, login and sign-up. Visitor mode: Operate.

## Brief

Renters find, book and open a real garage door from the phone; hosts list garages and pair doors.
Replace the "Garage at night" world entirely; keep every feature, flow, text and state. The door
moment on an active booking is the memorable moment. Bulgarian first, English second.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every garage is a painted door in the row behind the panel blocks. The category default is
a dark map app with neutral cards and one brand colour; GarageGo refuses that: each garage carries
its own door, and the app's identity is the row of doors.

OWN-WORLD: Light concrete ground (#EDEBE4) with near-white rows and sheets, graphite ink. Door
paints, deepened for contrast, are the only saturated colours: mint, ochre, oxide red, sky blue,
olive, plum, each garage given one for good. Door tiles carry horizontal corrugation lines and a
stencilled size letter (М, С, Г / S, M, L) set in a condensed heavy face. One ochre action colour
for the forward and Open buttons; graphite primary buttons; hairline-ruled flat lists.

STORY: The renter recognises a garage by its door colour across Find, the garage page and
Bookings, books it, and at the garage taps Open and watches that same door roll up.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Find, top to bottom: GarageGo wordmark with a small door mark, the headline, the
plain-language search field with examples and the search button, then the list of garages as
rows, each led by a 58pt door tile in its paint with its size stencilled, title and area, hourly
price right-aligned. The floating tab bar stays at the bottom.

FORM: grounded candidate 1 of 7 (Impeccable's pick, chosen by the user over the roll), seed:69a42381e56eab59.
Signature interaction: the active booking's door panel shows the garage's own painted door, which
rolls up on Open and down on Close. Motion grammar: one roll per state change, ease-out, crossfade
under Reduce Motion; no other decorative motion.
